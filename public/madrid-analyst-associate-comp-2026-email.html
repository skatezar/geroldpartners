Analyst 1 Compensation

03 — Key Observations

Key Observations

  • Madrid comp is below London and Paris — typically 15–30% lower in total comp at equivalent levels. This is narrowing at the top end as international banks apply more consistent global pay grids.
  • Big 4 are losing talent faster — the gap has widened to the point where lateral moves to Spanish boutiques or international banks at analyst level are increasingly common after 12–18 months.
  • Credit funds compete harder at associate — carry and co-invest access from Associate 2 onwards is a meaningful differentiator that is difficult to value but increasingly understood by candidates.
  • Bonus variability is highest at boutiques and PE — in strong deal years the top end understates what is paid; lean years compress the range.
  • Retention is a growing issue at Spanish boutiques — several have introduced informal retention packages at associate level to compete with outbound offers from international banks and PE funds.
