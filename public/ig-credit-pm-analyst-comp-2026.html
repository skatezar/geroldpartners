Methodology
Data & Methodology

Data source: Gerold & Partners proprietary compensation database, compiled from direct candidate conversations, offer negotiations, and placement data across FY2025. All data points are individually verified through direct dialogue with candidates or hiring managers — no survey data or extrapolated estimates are used.

Sample: 32 total prints — 30 analyst prints across three seniority bands (11 junior, 7 mid, 12 senior) and 17 PM prints across two bands (approximately 8–9 per band). Given sample sizes, P25–P75 (box) figures are more statistically robust than P10/P90 whiskers, particularly for analyst bands. Readers should treat percentile extremes as directional rather than precise.

Scope: London-based buy-side roles only. Employer types include insurance asset managers (Aviva Investors, M&G, L&G IM, Aegon AM, Royal London AM), traditional asset managers (Schroders, abrdn, Invesco, Fidelity, Jupiter, Artemis), and specialist IG / securitised credit managers. Leveraged loan PMs, high yield PMs, private credit, and direct lending roles are explicitly excluded — these carry materially higher total comp (typically 25–50% premium at PM level) and would distort the distribution if included.

Compensation definition: Total compensation = base salary + annual discretionary bonus (cash component + deferred fund shares, where applicable). Carry is excluded and not applicable: all funds in this dataset operate on management fees only (20–40bps AUM), with no performance fee / carry structures. All figures are gross, pre-tax, GBP.

Confidentiality: All individual data points are anonymised. No individual, employer, or specific salary can be identified from the distributions presented. This report is shared in confidence and is intended solely for the recipient.
